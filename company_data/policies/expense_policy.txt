starX WORKSPACE — EXPENSE POLICY (DEMO)
Version: 1.0

Employees must provide a clear business purpose for each expense claim.
Expenses above ₹7,500 require manager approval before reimbursement.
Hotel claims must identify the dates and business purpose of the stay.
The AI employee may investigate, retrieve documents, and prepare a proposed action without approval.
The AI employee must not submit a write action without explicit human confirmation.
After submitting a request, the AI employee must revisit the company workspace and independently verify the resulting status.
The final report must include the expense ID, relevant policy evidence, observed status, and any errors.
